Who This Covers

What We Collect On The Website

The only information this site collects is what you type into a contact form — on the home page, the Priority Response page, or the piece-inquiry form on The Collection page. Depending on the form, that's your name, email, phone number, project type, and message.

Those forms are handled by Formspree, a third-party form processor, which emails the submission straight to Dylan. We don't run analytics or tracking scripts on this site, and we don't use cookies to track you. The site does load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch those font files when the page loads.

How We Use It

We use what you send us for one thing: to respond to your inquiry. We don't sell your information, rent it, or share it with third parties for marketing. If your project moves forward, the same contact details get used for quoting, scheduling, and invoicing that job.

SMS Terms & Conditions

DNK Fabrication runs an internal text alert system that sends operational notifications from our Twilio number, (806) 404-0947, to the business owner's own phone. These are not customer-facing messages — no customer or third party ever receives a text from this number.

What The Alerts Are

  • Notice of an incoming customer email
  • Invoice payment status (paid, overdue, etc.)
  • Job status and machine status updates from the shop

What The Program Is Not

This is not a marketing program. No promotional messages are sent, and no mobile number or message content is shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing purposes.

Frequency & Cost

Message frequency varies with shop activity. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan.
