First Friday Art Trail

Sheet-Metal Sculpture On Show

Albers Variations by Terah Maher, Assistant Professor, TTU Huckabee College of Architecture
November 5&J Exhibition, CASP's 5&J Gallery, 1106 5th St, Lubbock
First Friday Art Trail, Fri Nov 6, 2026, 6pm

On Friday night the work went up in front of the public. We laser cut, formed and assembled the sculptures from the artist's own files, in 20 gauge cold rolled steel, with the slotted bends formed by hand.

We also built a stand for each piece. Six sculptures and six stands in all, and the artist was thrilled with how they turned out.

Laser cutting, hand forming, assembly, custom stands.

Custom Furniture

Custom Hanging Bench

Built for Gerardo Bautista, Eco Design Guy · delivered Aug 14, 2026

Gerardo Bautista is the founder of Eco Design Guy, an interdisciplinary studio whose products have been featured in Architectural Digest, HGTV and Dwell Magazine. We built this rope-hung porch bench to his drawing: about 77 in long and 34 in deep, with an 11 ga steel tube frame, flat bar slats for the seat, laser-welded panels and four corner eye bolts to hang it from.

We made it for a 73 by 33 in cushion and sent it out powder coated. The finish is what this piece is about, so we took our time on it.

Steel tube frame, laser welding, powder coat.

Educational Robotics Support

Texas Tech robotics teams · Liberty High School Robotics, Lubbock-Cooper ISD

We like helping young builders get real parts for their robots. A Texas Tech robotics team came to us for laser-cut steel blades, and the Liberty High School robotics team brought us chassis parts for their robot, cut from 1/8 in aluminum.

If you coach or run a robotics program, send us your files and tell us what you're building. We're glad to help.

Laser cutting in steel and aluminum.
